Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to My_app, a private, personal-use application operated by CHRISTIAN WOLTER GRANN (“I”, “me”, or “my”).

What the application does

My_app is a read-only personal finance tool. It may request access to account information, including balances and transactions, from my own bank accounts through Enable Banking.

Information processed

The application may process account identifiers, account names, balances, currencies, transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, and other information returned by the bank. Authentication is completed through the bank’s own authentication process.

How information is used

The information is used only to display and analyse my own account information for personal purposes. It is not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Sharing

Information may be transmitted to the banking and technical services required to provide the application, including Enable Banking and the AI or MCP client used by me. I do not intentionally share the information with other parties.

Retention and security

I take reasonable steps to protect credentials and account information, including keeping private keys and configuration files confidential. Information is retained only for as long as needed for my personal use. I will delete it when it is no longer needed.

Third-party services

Bank authentication and account access are provided by third parties. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

Contact

For questions about this application, contact cwg@live.dk.

Changes

I may update this policy when the application or its processing changes. The latest version will be published on this page.
